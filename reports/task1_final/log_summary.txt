Interpretation and recorded limitations
Gaussian beta=0.5 has the best observed MAE accuracy (75.66% linear, 65.98% KNN).
Uniform beta=1 lowers MAE classification accuracy despite stronger smoothing.
SiT results concern features only; generation quality was not established.
Each variant uses one training seed. Runs used an H200 with a 7 GiB allocator cap;
execution on a physical RTX 3070 remains unverified.
Some historical extraction metadata says BF16; actual default CUDA autocast was
FP16, as documented in audit.json. Recorded scores are unchanged.
Earlier reports/task1_200ep/ uses subset probes; task1_final/ is authoritative.
